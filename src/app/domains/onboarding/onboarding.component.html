Welcome Aboard: Your Onboarding Journey

1
Pre-start Preparation Today
  • Collect necessary documents;
  • Provide access to internal systems;
  • Introduction to clinic structure, values, and policies;
2
First Working Week 1-5 business days
  • Welcome meeting with mentor/HR
  • Tour of the clinic
  • Distribution of equipment and uniform
  • Safety and hygiene briefing
3
2-3 Working Week 5-10 business days
  • Introduction to workflows, systems, and documentation
  • Shadowing experienced staff
  • Starting simple tasks under supervision
4
Adaptation 1-3 months
  • Regular feedback sessions
  • Gradual increase in responsibilities
  • Performance review and final adaptation meeting
